Premium Materials

Built to Last.
Built to Perform.

We chose every component for durability, performance, and real-world gym use.

Roller Core

EPP Foam

Expanded Polypropylene

The highest-density roller foam available. Maintains shape under repeated heavy use, delivers consistent therapeutic pressure, and resists compression fatigue.

  • Density: 60–90 kg/m³
  • Shore Hardness: ~50D
  • Temperature resistant: −20°C to 80°C
Bag Shell

600D Polyester

+ 1000D Nylon Reinforcement

Industry-standard 600D polyester resists abrasion, dries quickly, and costs 30% less than equivalent nylon — while 1000D nylon reinforces all high-stress corners and strap attachment points.

  • Weight: ~220 gsm
  • Abrasion resistance: ASTM D4966
  • Water-repellent DWR coating
Closure

YKK #8 Zippers

Dual Coil, Water-Resistant

The global gold standard in zipper hardware. Rated for 50,000+ open/close cycles with a water-repellent finish that keeps your gear dry in light rain or spills.

  • Tape: Polyester weave
  • Slider: Zinc alloy, black nickel finish
  • Two pulls for open-from-either-end
Carry System

EVA-Padded Straps

1.5" Nylon Webbing

Heavy-duty 1.5" nylon webbing shoulder straps with 6mm closed-cell EVA foam padding, covered in breathable 3D spacer mesh to reduce sweat and hot spots.

  • Strap width: 38mm / 1.5 in
  • Padding: 6mm EVA foam
  • Load rated to 25 lbs / 11.3 kg
Hardware

Ladder-Lock Buckles

+ Steel D-Rings

Polypropylene ladder-lock buckles for strap length adjustment and quick-release. Stainless steel D-rings at all four strap attachment points provide industrial-grade security.

  • Buckle: PP, rated 80 kg
  • D-rings: 304 stainless steel
  • Quick-release: < 2 second detach
3D-Printed

TPU End Caps

Thermoplastic Polyurethane

Custom-designed TPU end caps protect the roller ends, conceal strap attachment hardware, and provide additional grip. Printable at home on any direct-drive FDM printer.

  • Material: TPU 95A Shore
  • Diameter: 152mm
  • Print temp: 230°C nozzle / 60°C bed
