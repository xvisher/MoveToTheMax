The interior is 127mm wide and approximately 400mm deep — about 3 litres. That's enough for a standard water bottle (lying on its side), a resistance band, a small towel, and a lightweight change of clothes. It won't replace a full gym bag, but it covers everything you need for a training session or commute.

The removable cap uses a dual-start helical thread combined with a bayonet lock and a silicone O-ring seal. One half-turn threads the cap on, then a quarter-turn engages the bayonet lock — a positive-click mechanism that prevents the cap rotating open under the vibration of rolling. The O-ring keeps moisture out.
