# MOVE2THEMAX — Dutch Legal Requirements Checklist

> E-commerce + Custom 3D Printing business, Netherlands, 3 employees.
> **DISCLAIMER: This is a structured research document. Always verify with a Dutch lawyer (advocaat) or accountant (boekhouder/accountant) before acting. Laws change.**

---

## 1. Business Registration (KVK)

### Legal Entity — Recommended: **BV (Besloten Vennootschap)**

| Entity | Pros | Cons | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BV** | Limited liability, credible, tax-efficient at profit >€100k, easier with multiple shareholders/partners | Min. €0.01 share capital (practical: €1–10k), more admin, accountant required | Recommended if 2+ founders or expecting growth |
| **VOF** (Vennootschap onder Firma) | Simple, cheap to set up, partners share control | Personal liability for all partners — debts are YOUR debts | 2–3 co-founders sharing risk |
| **Eenmanszaak** | Simplest, €0 setup cost, minimal admin | Full personal liability, less credible for B2B | Solo founder, low risk |

### KVK Registration Steps:
- [ ] Book appointment at KVK.nl (€75 one-time registration fee for BV)
- [ ] BV requires a **notaris** (notary) for deed of incorporation — cost: **€500–1500** depending on notary
- [ ] Prepare articles of association (statuten) — notary handles this
- [ ] Open business bank account (required before KVK registration is finalised for BV)
- [ ] KVK issues **KVK-nummer** (8-digit) — needed on all invoices and website
- [ ] KVK issues **RSIN** (tax entity number for Belastingdienst)

**Timeline:** ~1–2 weeks from notary appointment to KVK registration.

## 2. Tax Requirements

### 2a. BTW (Omzetbelasting / VAT)

- [ ] Register for BTW at **Belastingdienst** (automatic after KVK registration for most businesses)
- [ ] Get **BTW-nummer** (format: NL123456789B01) — display on all invoices and website
- [ ] Apply correct BTW rates:
  - **21%** — standard rate (applies to most 3D printed products, accessories)
  - **9%** — reduced rate (NOT applicable to your products)
  - **0%** — exports outside EU
- [ ] BTW return filing: **quarterly** (kwartaalaangifte) via Belastingdienst online portal
  - Q1: filed by April 30
  - Q2: filed by July 31
  - Q3: filed by October 31
  - Q4: filed by January 31 following year
- [ ] Pay BTW due within 1 month of quarter end

### 2b. EU VAT OSS (One Stop Shop) — for sales to EU consumers

- [ ] If selling to consumers in other EU countries: register for **VAT OSS** via Belastingdienst
- [ ] OSS thresholds: >€10,000/year total EU cross-border sales triggers OSS obligation
- [ ] OSS return filed quarterly — covers all EU countries in one filing
- [ ] Shopify + Moneybird handle OSS calculations automatically

### 2c. Vennootschapsbelasting (Corporate Tax) — for BV

- [ ] File annual **Vpb return** (vennootschapsbelastingaangifte)
- [ ] Rates (2025): **19%** on first €200,000 profit, **25.8%** above €200,000
- [ ] Deadline: within 6 months after financial year end (June 30 for calendar year)
- [ ] Provisional Vpb assessments may be issued — pay quarterly to avoid interest

### 2d. Loonheffing (Payroll Tax) — for 3 employees

- [ ] Register as **inhoudingsplichtige** (withholding agent) at Belastingdienst before first payroll
- [ ] Monthly loonheffing filing and payment (by the 31st of following month)
- [ ] Loonheffing = loonbelasting (income tax withheld) + premies volksverzekeringen (AOW, ANW, WLZ)
- [ ] Also pay: **werkgeverspremies** — WW (unemployment), WIA (disability), ZVW (health insurance contribution)
- [ ] Use payroll software (Employes.nl or accountant) — do NOT do this manually

### 2e. Inkomstenbelasting (if Eenmanszaak/VOF, not BV)
- [ ] File annual IB return (aangifte inkomstenbelasting)
- [ ] Zelfstandigenaftrek (self-employment deduction) if >1,225 hours/year in business
- [ ] MKB-winstvrijstelling: 12.7% deduction on profit

## 3. Employment Law (3 Employees)

### Contracts
- [ ] Written **arbeidscontract** required for each employee (legal requirement)
- [ ] Must include: name, start date, job description, salary, hours, probation period, notice period, vacation days
- [ ] **Proeftijd** (probation): max 1 month for contracts <2 years; max 2 months for indefinite contracts
- [ ] Can start with **tijdelijk contract** (fixed-term) — max 3 consecutive fixed-term contracts over max 3 years, then must offer permanent (Wet Werk en Zekerheid / Ketenregeling)

### CAO (Collective Labor Agreement)
- [ ] Check if a **CAO** applies to your sector — for e-commerce/retail: possibly **CAO Detailhandel** or **CAO Zelfstandige Groothandel**
- [ ] 3D printing manufacturing may fall under **CAO Metaal en Techniek** or no CAO
- [ ] Consult with a Dutch employment lawyer or HR advisor to determine applicability

### Minimum Wage (Wettelijk Minimumloon — WML)
- [ ] **2025 minimum wage:** ~€13.68/hour (updated July 1 and Jan 1 each year — check CBS.nl for current rate)
- [ ] All employees must earn at least WML regardless of contract type

### Required Employee Benefits
- [ ] **Vakantiedagen:** minimum 4 × weekly working hours per year (20 days for full-time)
- [ ] **Vakantiegeld:** minimum 8% of annual gross salary, paid by June 1 each year
- [ ] **Ziekteverzuim:** employer pays 70% of salary (minimum WML) for up to 2 years of illness
  - [ ] Consider **ziekteverzuimverzekering** (sick pay insurance) to cover this risk
- [ ] **Pensioen:** No statutory obligation to offer pension for all employers (check CAO), but strongly recommended

### UWV Registration
- [ ] Register all employees with **UWV** (employee insurance institute)
- [ ] Report employee start dates via loonheffing system
- [ ] UWV handles WW (unemployment) and WIA (disability) premiums

## 4. Product Liability & Consumer Protection

### EU Product Liability (for 3D printed goods)
- [ ] **EU Product Liability Directive** (updated 2024) applies to all products sold in EU
- [ ] As manufacturer of 3D printed products, you bear product liability — carry insurance
- [ ] Custom products made to spec: liability still applies if the product causes harm
- [ ] Keep records of: materials used, print settings, quality checks per order

### CE Marking
- [ ] Required for specific product categories (electronics, toys, machinery, medical devices)
- [ ] **Decorative/accessory 3D prints** (like gym bag parts): likely NOT required
- [ ] If printing functional mechanical parts or items that touch skin: consult a CE specialist
- [ ] Do NOT affix CE mark unless you've done proper conformity assessment

### Distance Selling Law (Wet koop op afstand / EU Consumer Rights Directive)
- [ ] **Herroepingsrecht:** consumers have **14 days** to return goods without reason
  - Exception: **custom-made products** (op maat gemaakt) are exempt from return right — clearly state this on your website
  - Standard stock items (non-custom): full 14-day return right applies
- [ ] Must inform consumers of return right before purchase (in terms & conditions + checkout)
- [ ] Return costs: you can charge customer if stated in T&Cs

### ACM (Autoriteit Consument & Markt)
- [ ] Comply with ACM price transparency rules: prices shown **including BTW** on consumer-facing pages
- [ ] No hidden fees at checkout
- [ ] Honest review practices — never fake reviews

### Algemene Voorwaarden (General Terms & Conditions)
- [ ] Draft T&Cs and have them reviewed by a Dutch lawyer
- [ ] Must cover: order process, payment, delivery times, returns, complaints, liability limitation, IP ownership
- [ ] T&Cs must be **available before purchase** (link at checkout + footer)
- [ ] Consumer T&Cs cannot exclude statutory rights (e.g., cannot waive warranty rights)
- [ ] **Garantie:** 2-year legal guarantee for consumers on products (Burgerlijk Wetboek 7:17)

## 5. E-commerce Law & Website Requirements

### Mandatory Website Information (Wet op het elektronisch handelsverkeer)
Your website **must display:**
- [ ] Full legal company name
- [ ] KVK-nummer
- [ ] BTW-nummer (for B2B transactions; optional but recommended for B2C)
- [ ] Physical business address (PO box alone is NOT sufficient)
- [ ] Email address (mandatory, phone optional)
- [ ] Prices including BTW (for consumer sales)

### AVG (GDPR) Compliance
- [ ] **Privacyverklaring** (Privacy Policy) on website — required, must be easy to find
  - Must explain: what data collected, why, how long kept, who it's shared with, user rights
- [ ] **Cookie consent:** implement a compliant cookie banner (no pre-ticked boxes)
  - Required consent for analytics cookies (Google Analytics), marketing cookies
  - Functional/necessary cookies: no consent needed
  - Use: Cookiebot, CookieYes, or Shopify's built-in consent
- [ ] **Data processing agreements** with all processors (Shopify, Mailchimp, Crisp, etc.) — most SaaS providers have standard DPAs
- [ ] Register with **AP (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens)** — no general registration required, but must have internal records of processing activities (verwerkingsregister)
- [ ] **Data breach:** must report to AP within 72 hours if personal data breach occurs

### Digital Services Act (DSA)
- [ ] Applies from 2024 to all online platforms selling in EU
- [ ] For small businesses (< 45M users): basic obligations only — T&Cs, contact point, cooperate with authorities
- [ ] Annual reporting if marketplace (not just a webshop)

## 6. 3D Printing Specific

### Intellectual Property
- [ ] Do NOT print copyrighted designs (characters, logos, patented shapes) without license
- [ ] If customers submit their own designs: get written confirmation they own the IP
- [ ] Add IP indemnification clause to your T&Cs
- [ ] Consider trademarking the MOVE2THEMAX brand at **EUIPO** (EU trademark, ~€850 for 1 class)

### Material Safety (REACH Regulation)
- [ ] Purchase filament from reputable suppliers with **REACH compliance documentation** (e.g., RoHS, REACH SVHC)
- [ ] Keep Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for all filament materials used
- [ ] Ventilation required in workspace — 3D printing emits UFPs and VOCs
- [ ] If printing food-contact or skin-contact items: additional material certifications needed

### Product Safety
- [ ] Follow **EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR)** — effective December 2024
- [ ] Document your quality control process for each product type
- [ ] Keep traceability records (which filament batch → which order)

## 7. Insurance Requirements

- [ ] **Bedrijfsaansprakelijkheidsverzekering (AVB):** Business liability insurance — ESSENTIAL
  - Covers: damage to customers/third parties caused by your business or products
  - Typical premium: €200–600/year for small business
  - Providers: Interpolis, Allianz, Hiscox, ABN AMRO Verzekeringen
- [ ] **Productaansprakelijkheidsverzekering:** Product liability insurance — HIGHLY RECOMMENDED for 3D printed goods
  - Often included in AVB, but verify explicitly for manufactured goods
- [ ] **Inventarisverzekering:** Contents/equipment insurance for printers (replacement value)
- [ ] **Zakelijk rechtsbijstand:** Legal assistance insurance — recommended
- [ ] For BV directors: **Bestuurdersaansprakelijkheidsverzekering (D&O)** — consider
- [ ] **Arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering (AOV):** for self-employed founders — highly recommended, otherwise no income if you can't work

## 8. Workspace & Operations

### Zoning (Bestemmingsplan)
- [ ] Check **bestemmingsplan** for your address via ruimtelijkeplannen.nl or your municipality
- [ ] Home workspace: permitted for light office/admin work; manufacturing (printers running all day) may require commercial zoning — check with gemeente
- [ ] Commercial unit: verify industrial/light industrial zoning permits small-scale manufacturing

### Environmental & Permits
- [ ] Small-scale 3D printing (< ~10 printers, hobby/light industrial): typically falls under **Activiteitenbesluit** — no separate environmental permit required
- [ ] File a **melding** (notification) to your municipality if required under Activiteitenbesluit
- [ ] Ventilation/air filtration recommended; some municipalities have local air quality rules

### Health & Safety (Arbobeleid)
- [ ] Required to have a written **arbobeleid** (health & safety policy) for employees
- [ ] Conduct a **RI&E** (Risico-inventarisatie & -evaluatie) — risk assessment of workplace
- [ ] RI&E must be certified by a **certified arbodeskundige** if you have employees
- [ ] Register with an **Arbodienst** (occupational health service) — mandatory with employees
  - Can be a basic "basiscontract" with an arboarts: ~€300–600/year
- [ ] 3D printing workspace: ensure adequate ventilation, ergonomic workstations, fire safety

## 9. Financial & Bookkeeping

### Boekhoudplicht (Bookkeeping Obligation)
- [ ] Retain all financial records for **7 years** (Dutch law requirement)
- [ ] Keep: invoices (in + out), bank statements, contracts, receipts
- [ ] Use Moneybird for digital bookkeeping (legally valid for Dutch purposes)

### Invoice Requirements (Factuurvereisten)
Every invoice must include:
- [ ] Your full company name and address
- [ ] Customer's name and address
- [ ] Invoice date
- [ ] Unique sequential invoice number
- [ ] KVK-nummer
- [ ] BTW-nummer
- [ ] Description of goods/services
- [ ] Quantity and unit price
- [ ] BTW rate applied and BTW amount
- [ ] Total amount excluding BTW
- [ ] Total amount including BTW
- [ ] Payment due date and bank account (IBAN)

### Business Bank Account
- [ ] Open a **zakelijke rekening** (business bank account) — required for BV, strongly recommended for all entities
- [ ] Options in NL: **Bunq Business** (cheapest, €9/mo), **Knab** (good for SME), **Rabobank/ING/ABN AMRO** (traditional, €10–15/mo)
- [ ] Keep personal and business finances strictly separate
